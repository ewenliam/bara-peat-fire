## Caveats

- The earliest detection is the first satellite detection, not the ignition point.
- Visiting a cluster is not extinguishing it.
- Tiers come from peat weighted risk with 8 P1 clusters for brigade now, 61 P2 clusters for village fire watch and 1518 P3 clusters for monitor.
- Only rivers are mapped within about 5.1 km each side from satellite imagery; drainage canals are too narrow to detect and small rivers can be missed, so no river seen does not mean no water at all.
- Forecasts are for 2026-10-31 and probabilities are model estimates, not certainties.
