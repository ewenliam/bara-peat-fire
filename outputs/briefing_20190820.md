## Caveats

- Earliest detection is the first satellite detection, not the ignition point.
- P1 means brigade now, P2 means village fire watch, P3 means monitor, with 18 P1 clusters, 117 P2 clusters and 2872 P3 clusters.
- Visiting a fire is not extinguishing it.
- River search used 10 m pixels over about 5.1 km each side of the centre, canals are not detected and small rivers can be missed, so no river seen does not mean no water at all.
- Spread forecast is for 1.1 km cells near fires of the last 7 days and new-fire watch is for 11 km quiet cells with no fire in the last 7 days.
